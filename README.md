# Found-r

BUILD FOUND-R

Design and build a production-ready SaaS platform called:

Found-r

Tagline:

Clarity Before Commitment

Use the attached Found-r logo as the master brand identity.

The platform helps people:

 Discover business opportunities

 Validate business ideas

 Launch businesses

 Operate businesses

 Grow businesses

The primary target audience is:

 Prospective business owners

 Existing business owners

 Franchise seekers

 Independent retailers

 Hospitality operators

 Trades

 Service businesses

 Multi-site operators

The platform should focus primarily on physical businesses (brick-and-mortar businesses).

PRODUCT VISION

Found-r should feel like:

Google Maps + McKinsey + LinkedIn + Crunchbase + Checkatrade + AI Business Coach

for anyone starting, buying, launching or growing a business.

The product must answer three questions:

What should I do next?

(Guidance)

What does the data say?

(Intelligence)

Who can help me?

(Marketplace)

Every screen should support at least one of these outcomes.
